There’s ice cream. And then there’s the pleasure of slowing down for gelato.

Gelato is typically churned with less air and served a little warmer than conventional ice cream. The result? A dense, soft texture that lets the flavour take centre stage.

Mr Gelato’s collection brings Italian inspiration together with familiar Indian fruit favourites, from mango to chikoo and custard apple.
