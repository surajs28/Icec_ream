# Mr Gelato website sample

## Platform
Web, static GitHub Pages.

## Purpose
Showcase Mr Gelato as the primary gelato brand with an appetising hero and complete menu. User approved the berry-red mockup composition and moving the coverflow to the homepage immediately after the hero.

## Constraints
Frontend-only sample, no live orders or newsletter storage. Preserve all 24 confirmed flavours and pint builder. No invented nutrition, dietary suitability, outlet details, testimonials or social handles. Illustrative photography. Existing static Tailwind website with React/TypeScript islands and shadcn aliases; technology choice delegated by user.

## Approved scope
Reference-matched hero, restrained motion, homepage carousel, consistent navigation and readable responsive spacing.
